Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, fill in your profile, upload a resume, apply for jobs, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, resume data, work history, skills, and location preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Match you with relevant job opportunities
  • Share your profile with recruiters when you apply for jobs
  • Send notifications about your applications and new job matches
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve the platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your personal information only in the following circumstances: with recruiters when you apply for their listed positions; with service providers who help operate the platform; when required by law or to protect our rights; with your explicit consent.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the platform, analytics cookies to understand usage patterns, and functional cookies to remember your preferences. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@sevajobs.in.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from your settings page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@sevajobs.in or use our contact form.
